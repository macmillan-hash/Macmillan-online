Short revision notes
MATHEMATICS
➗

Algebra

Algebra uses letters to represent unknown numbers.

Example: x + 5 = 12
Subtract 5 from both sides: x = 7.

Remember: Do the same operation to both sides of an equation.

BIOLOGY
🌱

Photosynthesis

Green plants make glucose using light energy.

Requirements: light, water, carbon dioxide and chlorophyll.

Products: glucose and oxygen.

CHEMISTRY
🧪

Acids and bases

Acids have pH below 7, neutral solutions have pH 7, and alkaline solutions have pH above 7.

Indicator: Blue litmus turns red in an acid.
PHYSICS
⚡

Electricity: Ohm's law

At constant temperature, current is proportional to voltage.

Formula: V = I × R
Voltage = current × resistance.
COMPUTER SCIENCE
💻

Computer hardware

Hardware means the physical parts of a computer.

Examples: keyboard, monitor, mouse, processor and printer.
GEOGRAPHY
🌍

Weather and climate

Weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions. Climate is the average weather pattern of a place over a long period.

ENGLISH
📖

Parts of speech

Nouns name people, places or things. Verbs show actions or states. Adjectives describe nouns.

HISTORY
🏛️

Using historical sources

Historians study written, oral, archaeological and visual sources to learn about the past.

AGRICULTURE
🌾

Soil conservation

Soil can be protected through contour ploughing, mulching, crop rotation and planting cover crops.
